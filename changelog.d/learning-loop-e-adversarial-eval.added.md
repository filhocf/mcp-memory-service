- Learning-loop adversarial eval (frente E): an offline, deterministic benchmark (scripts/benchmarks/adversarial_eval.py) that emits three commit-comparable numbers — MRR (reusing the LoCoMo harness, vs baseline 0.4140), taxa_freshness (fraction of versioned pairs where injection ranks the newer belief first — measures frente C), and taxa_forgetting (fraction of injected-but-unused beliefs whose confidence degrades through the real penalty cycle — measures frente B). Runs with no network and no LLM; discriminates feature-on vs feature-off (freshness 1.0/0.0, forgetting 1.0/0.0).
